## [Main] - 2026/10/29

### Added

- MAJOR Added a finite volume subcell limiter for inviscid compressible flows based on the publications
    1. Vilar, F. (2019). A posteriori correction of high-order discontinuous Galerkin scheme through subcell finite volume formulation and flux reconstruction. Journal of Computational Physics, 387, 245-279.
    2. Sonntag, M., Munz, C.-D. (2016). Efficient Parallelization of a Shock Capturing for Discontinuous Galerkin Methods using Finite Volume Sub-cells. Journal of Scientific Computing, 70, 1262-1289.
It is based on the following publication: Adams, T., Giani, S., & Coombs, W. M. (2019). A high-order elliptic PDE based level set reinitialisation method using a discontinuous Galerkin discretisation. Journal of Computational Physics, 379, 373-391.

- MAJOR Provide an FESubcellEvaluation and FESubcellFaceEvaluation infrastructure to compute the finite volume subcell values on DG cells.

- MAJOR Add a troubled cell marker algorithm which identifies cells with nonphysical solutions by a physical and numerical admissibility detection algorithm.
