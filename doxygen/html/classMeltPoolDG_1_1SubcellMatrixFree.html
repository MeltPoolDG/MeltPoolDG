Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename number>
class MeltPoolDG::SubcellMatrixFree< dim, number >

General Description

This class precomputes the data for FESubcellEvaluation and FESubcellFaceEvaluation that does not change between the calls of a matrix-free loop, analogous to dealii::MatrixFree for dealii::FEEvaluation. It holds two kinds of data:

  • The index data and the geometry on the unit cell that are the same for all cells, see SubcellShapeInfo, including the haloed layout used for the subcell stencils, see SubcellIndexInfo.
  • The neighbors across each face of the locally owned and the ghost cells held by the dealii::MatrixFree object, needed for the subcell stencils that reach over the faces of a cell.

Neighbor Types

The neighbor of a cell across a face is one of the following:

  • held: The neighbor is held by the dealii::MatrixFree object, either as a locally owned or as a ghost cell. Its DoF values can be read with dealii::FEEvaluation::reinit() for the cell indices given in FaceNeighbors::cell_ids.
  • extra: The neighbor is a ghost cell that is not held by the dealii::MatrixFree object. Its DoF values are exchanged by this class in update_ghost_values() and read with read_extra_cell_dof_values().
  • boundary: The face lies at the domain boundary.

When MatrixFree::AdditionalData::hold_all_faces_to_owned_cells = true, all face neighbors of the locally owned cells are held. Extra neighbors only occur for the ghost cells, mainly at corners of the partition boundary. There, the face neighbor of a ghost cell in the above described haloed layout can be an edge neighbor (in 2D a vertex neighbor) of a locally owned cell, which dealii::MatrixFree does not hold. The face neighbors of the ghost cells are needed when computing gradients from the subcell values, since the corresponding subcell stencil on a face reaches over the lateral faces of both adjacent cells.

Requirements

The class currently requires

  • MatrixFree::AdditionalData::hold_all_faces_to_owned_cells = true with face integrals,
  • a DG element in the DoFHandler, i.e. no DoFs shared between cells,
  • Cartesian cells without hanging nodes,
  • faces in standard orientation and aligned neighbors, i.e. the face face_no of a cell is the face face_no ^ 1 of its neighbor, including periodic neighbors.
