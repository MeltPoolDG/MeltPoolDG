Detailed Description

template<int dim, int n_components, typename number>
struct MeltPoolDG::SubcellNumericalAdmissibility< dim, n_components, number >::DeferredSubcell

A subcell at the boundary of a locally owned cell whose candidate value violates the bounds from the subcells of its own cell. Whether it is admissible is decided in evaluate(), once the face layer extrema of the neighboring cells are available.
