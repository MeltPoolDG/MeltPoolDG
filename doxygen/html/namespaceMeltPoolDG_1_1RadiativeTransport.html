Detailed Description

Types and functions shared by the ray tracers in MeltPoolDG::RadiativeTransport (RayTracing: marching cubes + ArborX, RayMarching: deal.II ParticleHandler).

This header must not depend on ArborX, Kokkos or any mesh data structure, so that every tracer can use it independently of its own dependencies.

Create the rays of a laser beam on a regular grid over the beam cross section.

The rays start on a grid with side length 2 * radius, centered at center and perpendicular to beam_direction, and propagate along beam_direction. The energy of a ray is the intensity at its origin times the area (3D) or length (2D) the ray represents. The rays are distributed round-robin over the MPI ranks: ray i is created on rank i % n_ranks and has the id i.

Parameters
centerCenter of the beam cross section.
beam_directionPropagation direction of the beam. It does not have to be normalized.
intensity_profileIntensity of the beam, evaluated at the ray origins.
radiusHalf side length of the sampled cross section.
spacingMaximum distance between neighboring rays. The actual distance is 2 * radius / ceil(2 * radius / spacing).
rankMPI rank on which the rays are created.
n_ranksNumber of MPI ranks.
Returns
The rays assigned to rank.
Exceptions
dealii::ExcMessageif radius or spacing is not positive, if rank is not smaller than n_ranks or if beam_direction is zero.
