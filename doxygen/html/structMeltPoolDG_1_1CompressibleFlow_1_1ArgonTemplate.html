Detailed Description

template<typename number>
struct MeltPoolDG::CompressibleFlow::ArgonTemplate< number >

Template for the properties of argon gas. The properties are taken from the following resources:

[1] Li et al. (2020), "Revealing transient powder-gas interaction in laser powder bed fusion process through multi-physics modeling and high-speed synchrotron x-ray imaging"

[2] Li et al. (2023), "Vapor-induced flow and its impact on powder entrainment in laser powder bed fusion"
