Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename number>
struct MeltPoolDG::SubcellShapeInfo< dim, number >

This struct stores the index data and the geometry on the unit cell of the subcells that are the same for all cells, analogous to the ShapeInfo of dealii::MatrixFree. The index data of the subcells of a cell and of the subcells adjacent to a face of a cell are stored in two instances of SubcellIndexInfo, see there for the numbering and the haloed layout.

template<int dim, typename number >
SubcellIndexInfo<dim - 1> MeltPoolDG::SubcellShapeInfo< dim, number >::face_index_info

Index data of the subcells adjacent to a face of a cell, numbered in the face coordinates, i.e. by the index of the subcell face in SubcellIndexInfo::face_to_cell_subcell_indices of cell_index_info. On a face normal to the direction d, the direction k of the face coordinates is the direction (d + 1 + k) % dim of the cell.
