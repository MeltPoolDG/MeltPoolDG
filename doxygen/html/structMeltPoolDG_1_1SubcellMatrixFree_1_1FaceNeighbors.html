Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename number>
struct MeltPoolDG::SubcellMatrixFree< dim, number >::FaceNeighbors

The neighbors across one face of n_lanes cells. Each lane holding a cell has exactly one of the entries cell_ids, extra_cell_ids and boundary_ids set, unless the neighbor is not available (see the class description). All entries of the other lanes are invalid.
