template<std::size_t n_species, typename VectorizedArrayType , typename ReadOnlyDofView , typename WritableDofView >

const std::array< VectorizedArrayType, n_species > &  dirichlet_boundary_values = {}

Sets the boundary value and gradient for partial densities in species transport. The boundary condition is only applied to the first n_species - 1 species, as it is assumed that the last species is a dependent variable and therefore is not part of the solution vector.

Parameters
w_pThe writable degree of freedom view for the values on the outer face.
w_mThe read-only degree of freedom view for the values on the inner face.
boundary_typeThe type of boundary condition to apply.
dirichlet_boundary_valuesThe prescribed partial densities for Dirichlet boundary conditions (optional, only used if boundary_type is dirichlet).
