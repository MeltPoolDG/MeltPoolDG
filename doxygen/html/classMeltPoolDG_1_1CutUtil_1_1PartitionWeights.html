Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class MeltPoolDG::CutUtil::PartitionWeights< dim >

Cell weights for the repartitioning of a parallel::distributed::Triangulation according to the location of the cells with respect to the level set.

The weights are evaluated with a dealii::NonMatching::MeshClassifier in update() and cached per active cell. Upon the first call of update(), a function returning the cached weights is connected to dealii::Triangulation::Signals::weight. It stays connected for the lifetime of this object.

With the default settings of parallel::distributed::Triangulation, p4est partitions the mesh according to the connected weights not only in an explicit repartitioning (see repartition_triangulation()), but in every call to execute_coarsening_and_refinement(). Such calls also occur if only the active FE indices change, e.g. in the solution transfer for a moved interface (see CutUtil::SolutionTransferOperator), where the level-set vector is not transferred. In these calls, no cells must be moved between the processes. Since the partitioning computed by p4est only depends on the global order of the cells and their weights, this is the case if the connected weights are exactly the ones p4est used for the current partitioning. Therefore, the weights are cached instead of evaluating the mesh classifier on the fly, and the cache is kept consistent with the partitioning as follows:

  • update() computes new weights from the current cell classification. They are used by p4est in the next change of the triangulation. Hence, update() must be called directly before an explicit repartitioning (see repartition_triangulation()).
  • The cached weights are transferred automatically in every change of the triangulation, i.e. in every call to execute_coarsening_and_refinement() and repartition(), exactly as p4est sees them: The children of a refined cell inherit the weight of their parent, and a coarsened cell gets the maximum weight of its former children (see get_weight()). This is implemented by attaching the weights to the cells (see dealii::Triangulation::register_data_attach()) in the signals pre_distributed_refinement and pre_distributed_repartition, and by unpacking them in the signals post_distributed_refinement and post_distributed_repartition of the triangulation.

Consequently, after adaptive mesh refinement the cached weights do not correspond to a fresh classification of the new cells, but they reproduce the partitioning computed during the refinement. They are updated in the next explicit repartitioning.

Note
update() must be called directly before each explicit repartitioning.

◆ update()

template<int dim>
void MeltPoolDG::CutUtil::PartitionWeights< dim >::update ( const dealii::NonMatching::MeshClassifier< dim > &  mesh_classifier)
inline

Compute and cache the weights of the locally owned active cells according to their location with respect to the level set.

The new weights are used by p4est in the next change of the triangulation. Therefore, this function must be called directly before an explicit repartitioning (see repartition_triangulation()).

Upon the first call, the function returning the cached weights is connected to the weight signal of the triangulation, and the transfer of the cached weights is connected to the refinement and repartitioning signals.

Parameters
mesh_classifierMesh classifier, which must be up-to-date with the current triangulation, i.e. reclassify() must have been called after the last change of the triangulation.
