Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename number>
class MeltPoolDG::Multiphase::InterfaceJumpModel< dim, number >

Model for the relevant parameters for the jump conditions at the phase interface, i.e., the evaporation mass flux, the temperature jump, and the laser heat source.

The evaporation mass flux and the temperature jump are either constant or computed with Knight's evaporation model.
