Detailed Description

template<int dim>
struct MeltPoolDG::SubcellIndexInfo< dim >

This struct stores the index data of the subcells of a dim-dimensional cell, which only depends on the number of subcells per direction. The subcells are numbered lexicographically with the first direction varying fastest, and the intra cell subcell faces as described in FESubcellEvaluation.

SubcellShapeInfo holds two instances: one for the subcells of a cell and one of dimension dim - 1 for the subcells adjacent to a face of a cell. For the latter, the cell is the face of the DG cell and its faces are the edges of this face in 3D and its vertices in 2D. For a zero-dimensional cell, i.e. the face of a one-dimensional cell, there is a single subcell and no faces.

Haloed Layout

Some subcell stencils, e.g. the central differences of the subcell gradients in FESubcellEvaluation, reach over the faces of a cell into the adjacent subcells of its face neighbors. To apply the same stencil to all subcells, the subcell data of a cell is then stored in a haloed vector, which pads the subcells of the cell by one halo subcell in front of and one behind the cell in each direction. With n = n_subcells_1d, a haloed vector holds (n + 2)^dim entries, and the subcell with the indices (i_0, ..., i_{dim-1}) is stored at position sum_d (i_d + 1) * (n + 2)^d, where the indices 0 and n+1 denote the halo. Hence, the neighbors of an entry in direction d lie (n + 2)^d positions below and above it.

The halo subcell across a face of the cell is the subcell of the face neighbor that is adjacent to this face. Only these face halo subcells are used. The entries across two or more faces of the cell, i.e. the corners in 2D and the edges and corners in 3D, are allocated but not used. The two-dimensional case with n == 3 looks as follows:

*     subcell indices          positions in a haloed vector
*
*                            +----+----+----+----+----+
*                            | 20 | 21 | 22 | 23 | 24 |
*    o===+===+===o           +----o====+====+====o----+
*    # 6 | 7 | 8 #           | 15 # 16 | 17 | 18 # 19 |
*    +---+---+---+           +----#----+----+----#----+
*    # 3 | 4 | 5 #           | 10 # 11 | 12 | 13 # 14 |
*    +---+---+---+           +----#----+----+----#----+
*    # 0 | 1 | 2 #           |  5 #  6 |  7 |  8 #  9 |
*    o===+===+===o           +----o====+====+====o----+
*                            |  0 |  1 |  2 |  3 |  4 |
*                            +----+----+----+----+----+
*
*    #, ==  boundary of the cell        o  vertices of the cell
*

The tables subcell_index_to_haloed_vector_index and subcell_index_to_haloed_neighbor_vector_index map the subcell indices to these positions.
